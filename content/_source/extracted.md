### **H1: Professional Teeth Whitening in Abuja**

A brighter smile starts with understanding what causes tooth discolouration and which treatment options are suitable for you.

DentistNearMeNG offers professional teeth whitening for patients who want to address certain types of tooth staining and improve the appearance of their natural teeth.

### **What Is Professional Teeth Whitening?**

Professional teeth whitening uses dental bleaching agents to lighten the colour of natural teeth. Depending on the method used, treatment may be carried out in the dental clinic or prescribed for use at home under professional guidance.

The appropriate approach depends on your oral health, the cause of discolouration and your treatment goals.

### **What Causes Tooth Discolouration?**

Tooth colour can change for several reasons, including:

-   Staining from tea, coffee and other foods or drinks.
-   Tobacco use.
-   Natural changes in tooth colour over time.
-   Certain medications or developmental conditions.
-   Trauma or changes affecting an individual tooth.
-   Plaque, tartar and surface deposits.

Not all discolouration responds to whitening. A dental assessment can help identify the cause.

### **Who May Be Suitable for Teeth Whitening?**

Whitening may be appropriate for some adults with healthy teeth and gums who want to reduce certain stains.

Patients with untreated cavities, gum disease, significant sensitivity or other dental problems may need treatment or further assessment before whitening.

Crowns, fillings and veneers do not whiten in the same way as natural tooth enamel, so existing dental restorations may affect the final appearance.

### **What Should I Expect?**

Your dentist may examine your teeth and gums, discuss your expectations and explain the available options. Treatment and the expected outcome depend on the whitening method and the type of staining.

Some patients experience temporary tooth sensitivity or gum irritation.

### **How Much Does Teeth Whitening Cost?**

### **Teeth Whitening FAQs**

**Does teeth whitening work on every stain?**

No. Some stains respond better than others, and certain types of discolouration may require alternative treatments.

**Is professional teeth whitening permanent?**

No. The duration of the result varies with diet, oral hygiene, tobacco use and individual factors. Maintenance may be required.

**Can I whiten teeth that have fillings or veneers?**

Natural teeth and dental restorations respond differently to whitening. Your dentist can explain how existing restorations may affect the result.

**Will teeth whitening cause sensitivity?**

Some patients experience temporary sensitivity. Discuss any existing sensitivity or dental problems with your dentist before treatment.

Keeping your teeth clean involves more than brushing alone. Professional scaling and polishing helps remove deposits that can accumulate on the teeth and around the gum line.

DentistNearMeNG lists scaling and polishing among its dental services for patients seeking professional cleaning and preventive oral care.

### **What Is Scaling and Polishing?**

Scaling is the removal of plaque and hardened deposits known as tartar or calculus from the teeth. Polishing helps remove certain surface stains and smooth the tooth surfaces after cleaning.

The procedure complements daily brushing and cleaning between the teeth; it does not replace them.

### **Why Is Professional Cleaning Important?**

Plaque can contribute to gum inflammation and tooth decay. When plaque hardens into tartar, it cannot be removed effectively by ordinary brushing.

Professional cleaning allows a dental professional to remove these deposits and assess the health of your teeth and gums.

### **What Happens During the Procedure?**

Your dental professional will assess your mouth and determine the appropriate cleaning approach. Instruments are used to remove deposits from the teeth, followed by polishing where indicated.

You may experience some temporary sensitivity, especially if your gums are inflamed or significant deposits are present.

### **How Much Does Scaling and Polishing Cost?**

### **Scaling and Polishing FAQs**

**How often should I have my teeth cleaned professionally?**

The recommended interval depends on your oral health, gum condition and risk factors. Your dentist can advise you on a suitable schedule.

**Does scaling damage the teeth?**

When properly performed, scaling removes deposits from the teeth. It may reveal spaces or tooth surfaces previously covered by tartar, which can make changes in the mouth more noticeable.

**Can scaling remove every type of tooth stain?**

It can remove many external deposits and surface stains, but it does not change the underlying colour of teeth in the same way as bleaching.

**Is scaling and polishing painful?**

Comfort varies with sensitivity, gum inflammation and the amount of deposit present. Tell your dental professional if you experience discomfort.
